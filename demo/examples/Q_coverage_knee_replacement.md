## 🟠 Covered, with conditions

Knee replacement is covered in principle, but it sits on the specified-procedure list, so it is not payable until 24 months of continuous cover are complete unless it results from an accident.

### Conditions and checks
🟠 **Listed procedure** — Joint replacement surgeries are on the specified list, which carries a 24-month waiting period (Excl02). *(Policy C.1.b, p.28)*
🟢 **Accident exception** — The waiting period does not apply to claims arising from an accident. *(Policy C.1.b, p.28)*
🟠 **Pre-existing disease** — If the knee condition is pre-existing, the longer of the two waiting periods applies (36 months by default). *(Policy C.1.a, p.28)*
🔹 **Room rent** — Room rent is payable up to the limit in the Policy Schedule for the plan. *(Policy B.1.1, p.11)*

- Ask for the policy's first inception date and the admission date, then run the waiting-period check.
- Ask whether the knee condition was diagnosed or treated before the policy started.
