## 🟠 Covered, with conditions

Associated Medical Expenses are hospital charges that vary by room category (e.g., consultation, OT, nursing, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen) and exclude pharmacy, consumables, implants/devices and diagnostics.

### Conditions and checks
🔹 **Included items** — Consultation fees; operation theatre; surgical appliances & nursing; anaesthesia; blood; oxygen. *(Policy A.1.2 Def. 5, p.8)*
🟠 **Excluded items** — Pharmacy and consumables; implants and medical devices; diagnostics are not associated expenses. *(Policy A.1.2 Def. 5, p.8)*
🔹 **Proportion application** — If insured chooses a higher room category, proportionate deduction applies to these expenses in the same ratio as room rent; ICU treated separately. *(Policy B.1.1.1 Note iii, p.12; A.1.2 Def. 5, p.8)*

- Check the hospital bill for line-by-line breakup of associated expenses and room rent.
